Privacy

Your data stays on your device, unless you choose otherwise.

Omni Hub doesn't require an account. Signing in with Google is optional and only syncs your own data across your own devices.

  • No analytics, no ads, nothing sold or shared.
  • The Vault is encrypted with your PIN; only already-encrypted data ever syncs.
  • Delete your account and everything synced from inside the app, any time.
